Not wireframed yet (lower stakes, design directly): journal browser, watchlist, risk-rule editor with effect preview, re-auth success/failure, pre-first-sync empty states. Open design questions these frames surface: (1) what the deliberate confirm on Approve physically is; (2) whether the collar band is a horizontal track (as here) or plotted on a mini price axis; (3) whether the halted state should mute the whole palette or just banner it.
