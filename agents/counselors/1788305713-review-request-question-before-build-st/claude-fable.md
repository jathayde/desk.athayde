# Step 7 design review: dashboard, system health, trade screens, Playwright

Based on the PRD §5.8 to §5.10, the flows doc, the wireframes, plan §2, §5, §6, §7, §8, and the models and jobs as they exist on disk today.

## Critical: the screens will expose these on day one

**1. Reviewing a bootstrapped trade is undone by the next positions sync.** `Trade#reconcile_plug!` calls `flag!(:bootstrapped)` whenever the plug quantity is positive (app/models/trade.rb:45), and `flag!` sets `needs_review: true` whenever it is currently false (app/models/trade.rb:29-33). `Journal::BootstrapJob` runs after every complete positions sync, so a legacy holding that John reviews at 10:00 is back in the queue at 10:15. Same for `exclude!`. Every bootstrapped holding stays bootstrapped for its whole life, so the review queue never drains. No spec covers "reviewed trade stays reviewed after a bootstrap run." Fix in the model before the screen: flag only when the plug leg is created or its quantity changes. A changed plug after review is a real discrepancy and should re-flag; an unchanged plug is not news.

**2. The money-market sweep fund is a position and a trade.** The account fixture holds SWVXX as a CASH_EQUIVALENT position with average price 1.0 (lib/schwab_fixtures/account.json:21). `Sync::PositionsJob` keeps every nonzero-quantity row (app/jobs/sync/positions_job.rb:13), `Journal::BootstrapJob` skips only zero quantity or nil average price (app/jobs/journal/bootstrap_job.rb:10), and `Position#protection` returns `:unprotected` for it. On the first real sync the dashboard shows a 2,110-share "unprotected" position, the unprotected count is off by one, open risk excludes it "with a reason," and a bootstrapped SWVXX trade for 2,110.5 shares sits at the top of the review queue. Filter CASH_EQUIVALENT at the positions sync, or at minimum skip it in bootstrap and in every dashboard query. Do it in the sync: the activity mapper already treats CASH_EQUIVALENT trades as sweeps, so the positions side should agree.

**3. The banner treats "never connected" as "expired."** The design branches on `SchwabToken.usable?`, which is false with no row at all. First login shows "Token expired. Sync paused. Re-authenticate," and `Sync::TickJob#pause!` has already written a pause reason that says "re-authenticate" (app/jobs/sync/tick_job.rb:49). Revoked is a third distinct case: Schwab rejected the refresh, and the copy should say so with `last_refresh_error`. `Schwab::TokenHealthJob#nag_reason` already has the right taxonomy, missing, revoked, expired, missed_sunday, expiring (app/jobs/schwab/token_health_job.rb:17-24), but it is private to the job. Extract it to `SchwabToken.status` and use it in the banner, the system page, the mailer, and `get_account_health` in step 8. That is the single method step 7 most needs and does not have.

**4. Skip has no durable meaning.** If Skip is navigation to the next id, a reload on the phone shows the same trade again, and the "N more to review" number never moves. Monarch's Skip means "later, not never." Add `trades.review_deferred_at`, touch it on Skip, and order the queue `review_deferred_at ASC NULLS FIRST, created_at ASC`. Never-skipped trades come first, then the oldest skips. No audit needed for Skip.

## Major: gaps the design has not decided

**5. Which account is the dashboard about?** Every query in the design says "the latest balance" and "positions," but the IRA syncs too, hourly, with balances and positions. Equity, open risk, cash, and the positions list must be scoped to `Account.trading`. The IRA gets one collapsed line with equity and its as-of stamp, or nothing in Phase 1. With zero trading accounts, which is the state on first login, the dashboard body is the setup card, not an empty positions list.

**6. Day change needs "prior snapshot" defined by date, not recency.** After the close plus 20 minutes today's snapshot exists, so "latest snapshot" makes the day change zero for the rest of the evening. Prior means the latest snapshot with `as_of` before today in New York. Flows since then come from `Snapshot.net_flows(account, after: prior.as_of, through: today)`, which already exists (app/models/snapshot.rb:38). On a Monday morning the prior is Friday; label it "since Fri close," not "today." With no prior snapshot, which will be true for the first one or two days, show the equity alone and say "first snapshot after today's close." Design that state; it is the first thing John sees.

**7. Distance to stop is nil exactly when it matters most.** `Position#distance_to_stop` returns nil unless `protected?` (app/models/position.rb:36-39). A partially covered or oversized stop has a price, and its distance is the actionable number; the coverage problem is the inline warning. Guard on `stop_price.present?` instead and keep `open_risk` as it is. Sort order for the list: unprotected, awaiting, and trailing-unknown first, then ascending distance, so a negative distance, price already through the stop, is the loudest row. The wireframe promises a distance bar; a bar needs a scale. Use the entry-to-stop distance as 100 percent and cap the fill, or drop the bar for Phase 1 and show the number.

**8. Staleness has no shared cadence.** "Older than its cadence" means the interval `Sync::TickJob` would pick right now for that account and feed: 15 or 5 minutes in session for a trading account, 60 otherwise, and 60 always for read-only accounts. Those constants live inside the job (app/jobs/sync/tick_job.rb:4-5). Extract `Account#sync_interval(feed)` so the tick and the view cannot disagree, and define stale as older than twice that interval plus the minute of slack, or any age when `sync_paused_reason` is set. The distance-to-stop number depends on two feeds: `last_price` from positions and `stop_price` from orders. Orders have no `synced_at`, and `updated_at` does not move when an upsert changes nothing, so the stop's as-of is the `finished_at` of the latest advanced-cursor orders `SyncRun` for the account. Show the older of the two stamps on that number. A failed run with a usable token has no banner state; the stale mark plus a one-line "last positions sync failed: error" under the header covers it.

**9. Two conditions at once hide the only call to action.** Precedence halted > expired means a manual halt buries the re-auth button while sync is dead. In Phase 1 the halt does nothing but paint a banner. Keep the precedence if you want, but the banner area must carry a secondary status line for any other active condition, and the re-auth link must survive under halted. The halted copy is also wrong as wireframed: Phase 1 has no tripped rule and no reset time. It reads "Halted by John, Mon 11:14a: reason. Clear it on the System page." Do not render the disabled "New proposal" button; there is no such entry point.

**10. The expiring rule will make Playwright flaky if it follows the mailer.** The mailer suppresses the 24-hour warning when Sunday's email covers it. If the banner does the same, whether it shows depends on the wall-clock weekday when the test runs. Use the plain `refresh_expiring_soon?` for the banner and add "Sunday's email will remind you" to the copy when covered. Deterministic, and honest.

**11. Setups cannot be created.** The `setups` table is seed-only (plan §1 row 11). On first login the review form's dropdown is empty and Save cannot tag anything. Add a "new setup" name field to the review form that creates the row. Also require nothing but the setup: a legacy holding may have no stop. The form must say "without a stop, R stays blank," and validate stop below average entry for a long, because `compute_initial_risk` silently returns nil otherwise (app/models/trade.rb:122-128) and John will not know why R is empty.

**12. The account-setup path is missing pieces.** Needs-setup means `account_type` nil, not `trading_enabled` false; a classified IRA is done. Three things to add: a validation that `trading_enabled` requires `taxable` (the PRD says trading other accounts is a non-goal); an `account_configured` audit kind, since this decides what gets journaled; and an immediate enqueue of positions, orders, and activities for that account on enable, because the tick otherwise waits up to an hour off-hours and `Journal::LinkFillsJob` only runs after an activities sync. The operator also has to tell two hashes apart. Show each account's latest equity and top symbols on the setup card; `Sync::CatchUpJob` has already synced both accounts read-only by then. Let the operator set `nickname` in the same form. The dashboard callout links to the system page's accounts section.

**13. "Not a trade" has no way back and a confusing label.** `exclude!` is one-way. Add "include in stats" on the trade page or accept it and say so. Label the button "Not a setup trade" so it does not read as "delete this."

## Per-screen checks and the Playwright plan

**Dashboard content vs Flow 1.** Equity, settled and unsettled cash, open risk with the excluded count, positions with distance first and P&L second: all producible once items 2, 5, 6, 7, and 8 land. Unrealized P&L is `market_value` minus `cost_basis`, both present when `average_price` is. `settled_cash` can be nil when both readings are nil (app/models/schwab/balances.rb:20-22); render "unavailable," never zero. The pending proposals row has nothing to link to; render "Proposals: Phase 2" without a link, or omit it. Two other empty states exist beyond pre-first-sync: a trading account with zero positions, all cash, says "No open positions," and token-present-but-no-sync-yet says "Waiting for the first sync" with the sync runs' status. The current dashboard view has the connect copy but no button (app/views/dashboard/show.html.erb:4).

**System page vs Flow 8 and §5.10.** Fillable now: token age and status, `refresh_expires_at`, next Sunday 18:00 marked with a loud line when the token dies before it, last success and latest status per feed per account from `SyncRun`, `gap_from` when set, pause reason, failed `SyncRun` count plus `SolidQueue::FailedExecution` in 7 days with truncated errors, shadow mode as a read-only indicator, accounts table with the inline form. Honest "not yet" rows: API spend shows "0 runs, briefs start in step 9," and the memory repo feed shows "never." No shadow-mode toggle in Phase 1; nothing to shadow. For the kill switch, use one word everywhere: the flows doc says "Pause system," the audit kinds say `manual_halt_*`, and "paused" already means sync. Call it Halt. Flow 8 puts the typed confirmation on unhalt; that is a required text field checked server-side, not a Stimulus confirm, which removes the only JS in the step. Set requires a reason and audits `manual_halt_set` with `Current.user`; clear audits `manual_halt_cleared`. "Halted by" comes from the latest `manual_halt_set` audit event; no new column.

**Trade screens vs Flow 4.** Post-mortem: two inputs, one line plus followed or improvised, on any closed trade. `post_mortem` normalizes blank to nil (app/models/trade.rb:18), so a followed flag with empty text leaves the trade in `missing_post_mortem`; require the text. When a closed trade also needs review, the review form renders first. Planned versus actual stop: for open trades the actual is the position's working stop looked up by account and instrument; for closed stopped-out trades it is the closing exit price. Trade index needs a `missing_post_mortem` filter, since the EOD email deep-links there. `journal_completed_at` is set by review, not by the post-mortem; decide what it means or stop writing it.

**Playwright.** Draw the scenarios route only inside `if Rails.env.test?` in routes, and guard again in the controller with `Rails.env.test? && Schwab.fake?`; development uses the fake adapter too and must not be resettable by a GET. The route must sign in a seeded user and set the session cookie, because the test mail adapter gives Playwright no inbox to read the magic link from. Call `Schwab::FakeClient.instance.reset!` on every scenario. Set the queue adapter explicitly for the Playwright server; test.rb does not set one, so `SchwabToken.issue!` enqueuing `Sync::CatchUpJob` would run on the async adapter in the background. Seed rows directly with the factories, not by driving jobs. Make times relative and unambiguous under either cadence: fresh is `Time.current`, stale is three hours ago, expiring is 22 hours out, expired is two days ago. Scenarios beyond the four banners: not connected, needs setup, one unprotected position (the fixture has none, both XYZ and DEF are protected), a failed sync with a usable token, the review queue with Skip persisting across reload, and the post-mortem save. Re-auth success needs the seeded account hash to match `Schwab::FakeClient::ACCOUNT_HASH` or `verify_accounts!` rejects it (app/models/schwab_token/reauth.rb:22-27).

**Hotwire Native.** Every screen a full page with its own URL and a short `content_for :title`, since the native nav bar uses it. No slide-over detail panel in Phase 1. No `data-turbo-confirm` or `window.confirm`; typed confirmation is server-side. Positions as a list of rows, not a table. Redirect after every POST. The OAuth redirect uses `allow_other_host`, which the native shell will need to open externally in Phase 2; note it in the path configuration, nothing to change now. `allow_browser versions: :modern` checks the user agent; the Hotwire Native user agent passes as Safari today, but keep it in mind when the shell ships. Refresh on load only; Turbo Streams for sync completions are Phase 2.

## Model and job changes before the screens

| Change | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Flag `bootstrapped` only when the plug is new or changed | `Trade#reconcile_plug!` | Item 1; the queue never drains otherwise |
| Skip CASH_EQUIVALENT positions | `Sync::PositionsJob` | Item 2 |
| `SchwabToken.status` extracted from the health job | `SchwabToken`, `TokenHealthJob` | Item 3; one taxonomy for banner, system page, mailer, MCP |
| `review_deferred_at` column and queue ordering | `trades`, `Trade.needing_review` | Item 4 |
| `Account#sync_interval(feed)` and `Account#last_sync(feed)` | `Account`, `Sync::TickJob` | Item 8 |
| `distance_to_stop` guarded on `stop_price`, not `protected?` | `Position` | Item 7 |
| `trading_enabled` requires `taxable`; `account_configured` audit kind; enqueue sync on enable | `Account`, `AuditEvent::KINDS`, system controller | Item 12 |
| Setup create-by-name on the review form | `TradesController#review` | Item 11 |
| Stop price validation with an explanatory error | `Trade#review!` | Item 11 |

Everything else in the step 7 design holds up against the PRD and flows. The order of work: items 1 and 2 first, since they are model bugs the screens will merely display; then 3, 8, and 4, since the views cannot be written without them; then the screens.
